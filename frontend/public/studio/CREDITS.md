# AeroVoxel visual assets

The landing artwork was produced with the built-in ImageGen tool. It depicts an
illustrative airfoil in a wind tunnel. The specimen thumbnails are cropped
AeroVoxel browser captures. The car model is credited in
[model attribution](../models/ATTRIBUTION.md).

See [DM Sans](dm-sans-OFL.txt) and [Space Grotesk](space-grotesk-OFL.txt).

## ImageGen prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Generate a single widescreen (landscape at least 3:2,
compose safely for 16:9) editorial cover photograph for the AeroVoxel educational
virtual wind tunnel website. Photorealistic, restrained research studio photography.
A beautifully machined silver NACA airfoil section with a clean cut end is mounted
horizontally on a single thin pylon inside a dark blue-black wind tunnel. Close
three-quarter angle from the leading edge; clear aerodynamic silhouette, brushed
aluminium highlights, delicate thin pale ice-blue illuminated smoke filaments
from left to right around the wing, precision test lab, faint slotted wind
tunnel wall far in background. Dominant wing and light on the right half and
center-right, a long usable area of nearly black uninterrupted low-contrast
negative space on the left for white headline overlaid by website CSS, bottom
enough room for fade. Midnight navy, soft white silver, minimal and real; no car,
no logos, no words or text, no UI, no numbers, no pressure legend, no invented
diagrams or measurements, no game neon or sci-fi tubes. High-end design editorial,
dramatic but readable geometry, one focal point.
